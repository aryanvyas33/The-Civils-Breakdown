GS2 · 2026 Q.8 15 Marks

"Cooperative federalism has become the new normal in India's governance. Critically examine with reference to the GST Council and Finance Commission."

GST Council Finance Commission Cooperative Federalism
Key Points:
- Bommai case 1994 is the landmark on federalism
- GST Council: Art 279A — unanimous decisions, centre has 1/3 voting power
- 15th FC devolution: 41% to states (Gadgil formula reformed)
- Critique: "cooperative" but centre holds power strings (Governor, Art 356)
