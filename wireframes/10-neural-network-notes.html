Your Consolidated Notes

Pre-conditions for Tropical Cyclone:

  • Large sea surface with temperature > 27°C.
  • Presence of the Coriolis force (enough to create a cyclonic vortex, absent at the equator).
  • Small variations in the vertical wind speed.
  • A pre-existing weak low-pressure area or low-level-cyclonic circulation.
  • Upper divergence above the sea level system.

Energy Source:

The energy that intensifies the storm comes from the condensation process in the towering cumulonimbus clouds, surrounding the centre of the storm.
